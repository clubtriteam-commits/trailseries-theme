# Race → season, 2012–2020 (audit H1, step 1)

Generated 2026-11-13 for the owner's check. Rules (owner, 2026-11-13): seasons 1–8 run October to
October; Birthday Run (mid-October) opens a season, iRan Run (~1 October) closes one; season 8 runs
October 2019 – December 2020; from 2021 a season is the calendar year (unchanged, not listed).

**Season key** = the year the season starts (2012 = season 1 … 2019 = season 8). October–December
races (Birthday Run, The Cactus Run, The Christmas Run, ОББ Vertical Run, the autumn-2012 races)
open the season of their own year; January–September races belong to the season that started the
year before; every 2020 race is season 8.

**Race year** comes from the page title ('18, 2018, run16-…) where present (`title`), otherwise
from the old page's publish date in the July 2026 export (`date …`); doubtful ones were checked
against the years written inside the old page.

**Stored now** = the `_tsr_season` values on the page's sections today (`—` = none; `×n` = number
of sections). Rows marked **change** get the new season in step 1.
